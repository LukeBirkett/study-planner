# Goals 
 - Understand the laws of matrix algebra (transposition, commutativity, existence of inverse)
- Understand the concept of the dot product of vectors, and be able to perform them by hand
- Understand the concept of a matrix inverse, and why you should never explicitly compute it in code
- Understand the abstract concept of a vector space
- Understand the concept of a system of linear equations, and how they can be represented and solved through matrix operations
- Start to understand the geometric intuition behind eigenvectors.
