#### Weekly Goals
This week contains non-assessed exposure to programming. 

##### Lecture
- Understand the concepts of: _program, script, REPL, CPU, machine code, interpreted language, compiled language, Just-in-time (JIT) compiled language_
- Understand the concept of memory allocation, and the differences in how memory is allocated when assigning variables through _pass-by-reference vs_ _pass-by-value_
- Understand how producing fast code in a compiled vs interpreted language leads to very different programming styles.
- Understand the concept of preallocation for concrete collections, and why it's a good thing.
- Understand the notion (but not the specifics!) of compiler optimisations.
- Understand the difference between parallel and serial code.
- Understand the difference between a _row-major_ and _column-major_ language, and why it's important.

##### Lab
- Understand, create and manipulate (lazy/concrete) iterable objects in Python/Julia
- Understand how to build and manipulate iterators to run on (/selected subsets of) iterables.
- Express iteration routines (e.g. summation) in mathematical syntax, for both unordered and ordered collections.
- Have a basic idea of functional programming styles and their advantages
- Understand and use the concepts of induction and recursion, both in programming and in proving/expressing mathematical concepts.
- Synthesise your knowledge to run a simple data processing pipeline as a nesting of lazy iterators.
