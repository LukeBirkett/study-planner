#### Goals 
- Understanding the conceptual foundations of probability and statistics is really foundational to properly applying AI and data science algorithms.
- Won't cover all the algorithms for data analysis on this course but provide the conceptual background to learn such topics. 
- Fundamentals of probability: what does a random variable/statistic/PDF really mean.
- Where are the subjective assumptions hidden when I use these as mathematical tools.
- Be comfortable with the concepts of: _probability space, random variable, probability mass/density function, statistic,_  and how they all relate to each other
- Understand Bayes' Theorem, and be able to derive it visually, and apply it to real problems
